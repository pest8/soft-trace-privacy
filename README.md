# Privacy Policy — SOFT TRACE

Last updated: May 2026

SOFT TRACE respects your privacy.

This game does not collect, store, or share personal information directly.

## Information Collection

SOFT TRACE does not require:

* account registration
* login
* personal profiles

The game can be played completely offline.

## Local Data Storage

The game may store the following information locally on your device:

* game progress
* level progress
* settings preferences
* sound/vibration settings

This data is stored only on your device using local storage and is not transmitted to external servers.

## Advertising

SOFT TRACE may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect anonymous data for advertising purposes, including:

* device information
* advertising identifiers
* general usage analytics

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children's Privacy

SOFT TRACE is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

## Security

We do not collect sensitive personal information.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Updates will be reflected on this page.
